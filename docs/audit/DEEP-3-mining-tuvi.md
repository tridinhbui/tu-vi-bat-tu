Conventions (stated in each builder): a "gặp" with no position is read in the palace itself; a named group ("Hỏa Linh Kình Đà") needs two
of its stars; brightness is read in the rule's own book (dignity@toan_thu for TT and 紫微斗數全書, dignity@khao_luan for KL; a star in the tam
phương on the chart's table, `palace.tp_rank`); the Hóa that a verse puts on a star is pinned by the year stem (Thiên Cơ hóa Kỵ = Mậu, Vũ Khúc
hóa Quyền = Canh, Thái Âm hóa Quyền = Mậu, Thiên Đồng hóa Lộc = Bính, Tham Lang hóa Lộc = Mậu); 独守 = the palace has no chính tinh.

Geometry found while reading (conditions corrected, not widened):
- Đại Hao and Tiểu Hao always face each other (Lộc Tồn ring +3 / +9): "Song Hao ở X" is the pair on X's axis, one of them in X.
  **The existing `TV_TH_CACH_MENH_CHUNG_THUY` (TH p.73) requires both in the Mệnh palace and therefore never fires** — follow-up below.
- Thiên Không and Đào Hoa always stand in one tam hợp group: KL's "Đào hoa Thiên không … tam hợp chiếu" is read as the pair together (in
  Mệnh or one tam hợp palace); read loosely it would hold for every Mệnh in that group (~25 % of charts).
- Long Trì and Bạch Hổ are always four palaces apart: "Long Hổ giáp Mệnh" (KL p.45) cannot occur; not made.
- Long Trì and Phượng Các share a palace only at Sửu / Mùi: "Phượng Long Mão Dậu" (TT p.76) cannot be read as printed; not made.

## Quality gates and what was trimmed after review

The first build (213 rules) passed the page checks but moved the narrative engine's own gates: the Mệnh chapter's Barnum (mean base rate of
the concepts voiced, `readable.test.ts`, baseline 40.5 %) rose to 45.2 %, and three unexplained contradictions and 60 uncited sentences
appeared in the coverage sweep. Fixed in the data, not in the prose:
- 31 Mệnh rules were taken back out (reason in each mined entry, `quotes_deep3_tuvi.json` 'skipped'): one-star traits the KL / TH star
  rules already give (Tử Vi, Thiên Đồng, Liêm Trinh, Thiên Tướng, Tả Phụ, Hữu Bật, Lộc Tồn, Kình Dương, Hóa Lộc, Hóa Khoa at Mệnh; the nữ
  mệnh one-star lines), lines whose only extra condition is "có cát / sát tinh trong tam phương" (true on most charts), Tuần / Triệt at
  Mệnh (already TV_TH_VERSE_MENH_TRIET_TUAN), and Khoa Quyền Lộc củng (already the special structure TAM_HOA_HOI_MENH).
- The cách-shaped lines became formation rules with no concept of their own, as the Thực Hành cách rules are: TT's hợp cách / phá cách
  by Mệnh branch, the KL hạ cách, Liên Giáp, Khốc Hư Tí Ngọ, Song Hao ở Mệnh, Tuần Triệt miếu địa. They are told in the Cách cục chapter.
- Wording the cue lexicon misread was rephrased (Khốc Hư "trước nghèo sau giàu" read as poverty; the Nô Bộc "đắc lực" lines read as the
  Mệnh concept social.support / social.isolated).
- Small code / content changes outside prose and reason: `src/interpret/generator.ts` (cite "TTH 2 八奴仆" → "Toàn Thư Hán văn, quyển 2,
  mục 八奴仆"), `src/interpret/semantic.ts` (school name of SRC_TOAN_THU_HAN), `src/interpret/validate.ts` (a "quyển N" locator counts as a
  page), `src/interpret/content/prose/minh_hoa.json` (the fin.self_made line said "từ sớm", which its own test forbids),
  `tools/interpret/make_benchmark.py` (control stars also exclude stars named by a verified cách; `--inert-only` re-picks only the controls,
  run once), `src/interpret/test/kg.test.ts` (the 紫微斗數全書 Nô Bộc / Tử Tức relations are not Mệnh relations).
After that: Barnum 40.1 %, strip 0.061, coverage sweep 16 warnings (baseline 15).
