## 4. Quality checks

- **Traceability.** Every conclusion lists rule ids, page citations and trigger facts. Every qualifier lists its page and, where a book word was interpreted, an open question.
- **No numeric scoring**: no score, average, percentage confidence or "lean" anywhere. The only number is TVH's own 40% rule, labelled as that book's method.
- **No repeated disclaimer**: one header per report, tested.
- **Forbidden phrases.** None of "chắc chắn sẽ", "nhất định sẽ", "suốt đời mang" or "lanh chanh" appears (tested).
- **Determinism**: identical output on repeat runs, tested on 4 edge charts (leap month, late Tý, both sides of Lập Xuân).

## 5. Known weaknesses

1. **Wording is template-based and repetitive.** Phrases such as "hai điều cùng đúng, đọc như một tiềm năng có giới hạn" recur. The chapter text is correct but not yet natural lay prose.
2. **Polarity of non-limit readings comes from word cues**, because the pack `effect` signs are wrong for ~55 rules (RL-4). It should be fixed at the builders.
3. **Only 9 qualifiers.** Most configurations still have no disqualifier, because none has been read on a page yet.
4. **The production cards still show raw findings.** The conclusion view is behind the flag.
5. **The decisions in INTERPRETATION-ENGINE-AUDIT.md §5 are open**, among them precedence, school choices and family claims.
6. **TS `src/interpret` and the flagged synthesis layer** still carry the old 4-pair stem table and their own taxonomies.
