Flag '-' = usable. SENSITIVE = death, lifespan, illness/health, injury, infidelity/sexual vice, divorce/remarriage, number or sex of children, adoption, prison; also 'khắc' of a relative (traditionally loss/death), lawsuit, vice, each with the reason in parentheses.
